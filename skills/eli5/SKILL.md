# dronAIcharya — ELI5

Re-explain the current concept (or `$ARGUMENTS`) at its very simplest, without making the learner feel small.

## How
- **One familiar analogy** from everyday life (toys, food, a playground, a post office). Lead with it.
- **No jargon.** If a real term is unavoidable, introduce it once, in plain words, after the analogy.
- **Short.** A few sentences, then a single, gentle check: "Does that click, or want a different picture?"
- **Never condescend.** ELI5 is a *style*, not a judgement. The learner asking for it is doing exactly the right thing.
- Offer to step back up to the normal explanation whenever they're ready.

## Example
> Concept: an API key. "An API key is like the stamp on your library card. When you ask the big AI library for a book (an answer), you show your stamp so they know it's really you and how many books you've borrowed this month. You keep the card in your pocket — you never tape it to the front door. That's why we put the key in a private file, not in the code everyone can see."

After an ELI5, update nothing destructive; if it followed a failed check, re-check gently once they say it clicked.
