# Code of Conduct

dronAIcharya is a tool for helping people learn, often absolute beginners. Our community should reflect that: patient, kind, and encouraging.

## Our standards
- **Be kind and patient.** No question is too basic. Meet people where they are.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, demeaning comments, or personal attacks. Critique ideas and code, never the person.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome contributors and learners of every background and skill level.
- **Be honest.** No misleading claims about what the tool or a contribution does.

## Not acceptable
Harassment, discrimination, trolling, insults, publishing others' private information, sexualized language or imagery, or any conduct that makes the space unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Scope
Applies in all project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may warn or ban repeat or serious offenders. To report a concern, open an issue (or a private report) on https://github.com/dibakshya01/dronAIcharya and a maintainer will follow up.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; kept short on purpose.
